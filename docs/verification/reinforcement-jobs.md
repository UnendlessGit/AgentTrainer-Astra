# Reinforcement jobs and native coordination

The compute role implements `train.reinforcement` using the real `PracticeEnvironment`, shared policy, recurrent collector, duration-aware GAE, PPO learner and immutable checkpoint writer. Its experience is labeled `practice_rollout`. It does not call the privileged demonstration oracle or require `verificationMode`. The practice environment has virtual time and no macOS screen/input access; asynchronous native rollout and reward integration remain separate work.

The request binds a starting checkpoint, environment configuration, nested PPO/return settings, context choices, destination UUID and total iteration target. Fresh runs unfreeze a BC checkpoint's temporarily frozen pretrained layers and start a new optimizer. Resume requires the exact saved model, environment, training and context identities, restores optimizer/counters/RNG, and starts a confirmed fresh episode. It does not restore a live world from recurrent state.

Cancellation during collection discards incomplete experience and saves unchanged learner weights. Cancellation during a PPO update restores the entire pre-update policy, optimizer and update count before checkpoint publication. A cancellation that arrives after the final completed unit may truthfully finish that unit. Terminal messages distinguish completed, cancelled and failed jobs; saved cancellation checkpoints carry `requiresEnvironmentReset=true`.

Progress includes collection counts, cumulative iterations/updates/decisions, elapsed time and the last completed iteration's reward, value/policy loss, KL, clipping, replay error and memory. The last iteration is carried across subsequent phases so output coalescing cannot erase it. Final history is capped at 1,000 records and 512 KiB, with an explicit dropped count, leaving room for the checkpoint/status within the 1 MiB wire limit. Iteration-local update counts never overwrite cumulative progress counters.

The native Training tab gives behavioral and reinforcement methods equal navigation. It supports pointing and delayed-memory practice, fresh pretrained initialization or a compatible linked checkpoint, original-configuration resume, actual iteration charts/table, immutable run configuration/results, checkpoint publication and cancellation that waits for the worker. A copied agent can begin RL from a shared BC checkpoint without changing the original selection or duplicating weights. The UI describes practice provenance and fresh-reset resume. External environment authoring/control integration and rendered UI/accessibility qualification remain open.

## September 11 update-admission review

The original guard above was insufficient to decide whether a *new* optimizer proposal was safe to publish. A deterministic sensitivity probe used the complete 34.64M default policy, pretrained encoder, 1280×720 source observations and the same 32 sampled decisions with four-step numerical replay chunks. Exact pre-update replay differed by at most `1.53e-5`. One nominal AdamW proposal produced sampled joint KL `1.635` with entropy coefficient zero and `1.682` with the default `.01`; both clipped 87.5% of sampled packet ratios. Thus entropy was not the primary source of this overshoot in this sample. Operation and timing likelihoods accounted for most mean log-probability movement; long packets amplified the shift. The probe took 56.8 seconds and peaked at 3.43 GB MLX memory. Its reproducible local report is `.local/verification/ppo-step-sensitivity.json`; it is a numerical diagnostic, not a full-rollout or task-learning benchmark.

The learner now validates each proposed optimizer step against **all valid decisions in the sealed rollout**, carrying current-policy recurrence from episode zero through bounded forward chunks. It uses the unchanged exact whole-packet likelihood, including END and every active argument. Candidate validation never uses stale behavior-state anchors or the training burn-in approximation. This is a sampled rollout bound, not a guarantee for unseen actions or environments.

When a proposal exceeds the run's KL target, the learner backtracks its complete AdamW parameter delta by `.5`, for at most eight backtracks. Both values are explicit saved configuration (`kl_backtrack_ratio`, `maximum_kl_backtracks`). Moments are computed once: the accepted scale acts as a per-step learning-rate multiplier, including weight decay, without incrementing optimizer clocks for each trial. Failure to admit any candidate restores that step's weights and optimizer state. Cancellation during a trial restores the entire pre-iteration learner/optimizer through the existing update transaction. Actor weights remain private and pending weights activate only at confirmed reset. Nominal learning rates and packet probabilities were not reduced or renormalized to conceal the overshoot.

`maximum_candidate_kl` reports proposals, including rejected trials; it is null if a trial was numerically nonfinite. `maximum_accepted_kl` and `maximum_sampled_kl` report only accepted proposals. `backtrack_count`, `minimum_step_scale` and `rejected_optimizer_steps` expose admission behavior. Reported ratio bounds come from accepted rollout replays, and `clip_fraction` is the final accepted replay's fraction. Losses remain the pre-update objectives of admitted gradient batches. The full-rollout forward checks have a real runtime cost, to be measured in representative training rather than hidden by reducing temporal or visual quality.

Progress now distinguishes `waiting_for_reset` (old-actor audit decisions, excluded from PPO counts) and `validating_update` (candidate scale and completed validation decisions). Both phases check cancellation. Existing saved reinforcement runs without the admission settings fail strict resume-configuration identity instead of silently changing their algorithm; their weights can initialize a fresh run.

The combined source RL math, trainer and asynchronous jobs suite passed **66 tests** after this change. Regressions exercise an overshooting first step with zero backtracking, accepted scaled proposals with only one optimizer-clock increment, exact accepted-KL replay, cancellation during candidate validation, and cancellation while waiting for a real reset. Native coordinator protocol fixtures separately passed **17 tests**; these do not claim rendered UI or live native control qualification.

Reloaded cancellation weights and **every optimizer tensor** matched the prior completed checkpoint, and the resumed optimizer advanced. This demonstrates admission and job/resume integration, not learned pointing success, multi-seed generalization or native control.

## Complete episodes and bounded rollout storage

Reinforcement configuration and saved training state now require **schema 2**. `rollout_decisions` is an explicit minimum: collect until that size is reached and the current episode actually ends. Every retained decision has the same frozen behavior policy. The learner sees the whole episode, including late rewards, and candidate validation still replays exact current-policy recurrence from episode zero. A pending actor snapshot activates only at the next confirmed reset. The old automatically discarded tail is gone; the compatibility diagnostic `audit_tail_decisions` remains zero. `finishing_episode` progress shows actual counts beyond the minimum. Saved cumulative decisions sum actual valid rollout sizes, rather than multiplying iterations by the minimum.

`learning/rollout_store.py` owns one private temporary file of exact compact BGRA bytes. Immutable frame references retain source shapes, byte extents and SHA-256 checksums. An LRU cache plus retained serialized metadata obeys `maximum_rollout_bytes` (default 4 GiB), with room reserved for one working source frame. Evicted pixels are read losslessly with bounded `pread`; no writable memory mapping, sparse allocation, resize or sampling reduction is used. The cache budget is not a bound on total process/GPU memory: current environment snapshots, preprocessing and learning tensors remain separate working sets.

`maximum_rollout_disk_bytes` defaults to 32 GiB and `maximum_rollout_decisions` to 65,536. Each frame write checks both the disk budget and a 10 GiB free-space reserve. Partial writes complete explicitly; failed writes, corrupt/truncated reads, metadata exhaustion or an episode exceeding the decision bound fail the run cleanly. They do not manufacture termination, zero rewards or a shorter successful rollout. The configured episode time limit supplies the ordinary boundary. Large episode/observation settings may require the user to adjust disk or decision budgets; admission errors explain the exhausted resource.

Jobs place the temporary spool beside their destination checkpoint. Successful update, explicit discard, stop, collection failure and job cancellation retire the file and cache. A failed or cancelled manual `update` keeps its sealed spool for a deliberate retry; discard then closes it. Accessing pixels after retirement gives an explicit error. Closing storage during a primary training failure preserves that primary error. Old schema-1 reinforcement resumes are rejected instead of silently changing their collection/training semantics; their compatible model weights can initialize a fresh run.

Focused tests cover byte-exact cache eviction/reload, checksum corruption, short writes, real write failure, per-frame disk reserves and capacity, cancellation cleanup, update retry ownership and configured decision/metadata limits. A real small-policy sparse-survival fixture sets the rollout minimum to three decisions and rewards only the ninth and final decision. All nine decisions enter GAE and a real PPO update; the late reward changes earlier return targets. Its raw bytes exceed the configured RAM-cache budget while peak cache/metadata stays within it. An asynchronous worker test records two seven-decision episodes from a four-decision minimum, verifies cumulative/checkpoint counts of 14, confirms progress beyond the minimum and checks that no spool remains.

The qualification script accepts `--episode-ms` so a deliberately short integration probe can preserve production temporal and visual settings while finishing real episodes. Its default remains the product's 120-second practice limit. Full default production-model/frozen-bundle learning with the new collector, longer sparse tasks, multi-seed learning quality and native asynchronous control remain release gates.

After schema-2 collection/storage integration, the combined RL math, rollout-store, recurrent trainer and asynchronous source-job suite passed **77 tests** on September 11, 2026. The late-reward and resource-fault checks use small numerical policies; they preserve actual environment decisions and training gradients but do not substitute for production-model learning quality measurements.
